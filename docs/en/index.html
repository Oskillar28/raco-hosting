In plain words

What is Jellyfin and what is it for?

Jellyfin is free, open-source software that turns a server into your own private "Netflix". It organises your films, series and music, fetches cover art and synopses, remembers where you left off and plays everything on your TV, phone or computer.

Unlike a paid platform, the content is yours: nothing you upload disappears from the catalogue and nobody tracks what you watch. I make sure the server stays on, up to date and reachable.

01

Upload your content. Your videos, music or photos are stored in your folder on the NAS.

02

Jellyfin organises it. It detects titles and adds artwork, languages and subtitles.

03

Watch it anywhere. Sign in with your account on your Smart TV, phone or browser.

04

Pick up where you left off. Start in the living room, finish on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Before you order

Do you provide the content on Jellyfin?

No. The service is the server: you upload your own content that you have the right to use (personal videos, music you've bought, etc.). I don't sell or share films or series.

What happens if there's a power or internet outage?

The NAS servers are connected to a UPS that keeps them running during short power cuts and shuts them down cleanly if the outage lasts longer. Your data is on RAID and backed up to the second NAS. If the internet goes down, the service comes back on its own once the connection is restored. I'll let you know about any planned maintenance.

Are my files private?

Yes. Each customer has their own user account and folder. Nobody else can access your space, and I don't browse it either.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. You pay month by month and can cancel whenever you want. Before closing your account, I'll let you download all your data.

How do I pay?

By Bizum or bank transfer, at the start of each month. I'll send you a summary of what your service includes.

How many players can the Minecraft server handle?

It's designed for small groups of up to ~10 players at the same time. If you need more capacity, get in touch and we'll look into it.
